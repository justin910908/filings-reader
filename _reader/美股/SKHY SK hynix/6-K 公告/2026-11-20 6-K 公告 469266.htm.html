Decision on Acquisition of Shares

On November 20, 2026, SK hynix Inc. (the “Company”) made a public disclosure with the Korea Exchange regarding its plans to acquire common shares of BCPE Pangea Cayman2, Ltd. (“SPC2”) through the exercise of conversion rights under certain convertible bonds issued by SPC2 (the “Convertible Bonds”) that are held by the Company and the purchase of SPC2’s outstanding common shares. Such disclosure amended and restated a previous disclosure filing made by the Company with the Korea Exchange on May 31, 2018. 10. Other Important Matters Relating to Investment Decision

 

    

This announcement relates to the Company’s acquisition of 129,000 common shares of SPC2 through the exercise of conversion rights under the Convertible Bonds held by the Company (the “CB Conversion Transaction”).

 

    

In addition, the Company plans to acquire 1,001 common shares of SPC2 held by BCPE Pangea Intermediate Holdings Cayman, L.P., an affiliate of Bain Capital, for a purchase price of JPY 100,000,000 (the “Share Purchase Transaction”).

 

    

Upon completion of the CB Conversion Transaction and the Share Purchase Transaction, the Company will ultimately acquire a total of 130,001 common shares of SPC2, through which the Company will hold an indirect equity interest of approximately 14.1% in the former semiconductor business of Toshiba Corporation (currently Kioxia Holdings Corporation).

 

    

The amount of share capital set forth in Item 1 above represents the total subscription amount for the Convertible Bonds. SPC2’s share capital as of the date of this report is JPY 100,000,000.

 

    

The amount of share capital set forth in Item 1 above and the aggregate acquisition value set forth in Item 2 above have been translated into Won using the base exchange rate of Won 991.39 per JPY 100 announced by Seoul Money Brokerage Services, Ltd. on May 30, 2018. The aggregate acquisition value set forth in Item 2 above represents the subscription amount for the Convertible Bonds and excludes the purchase price of JPY 100,000,000 for the 1,001 common shares under the Share Purchase Transaction and expected interest income of JPY 1,101,918,000 arising from the holding of the Convertible Bonds.

 

    

The Company’s total shareholders’ equity set forth in Item 2 above and the Company’s total assets set forth in Item 7 above are based on the Company’s consolidated financial statements as of December 31, 2017 prepared in accordance with the International Financial Reporting Standards as adopted by Korea.

 

    

The scheduled acquisition date set forth in Item 6 above remains subject to change. The actual acquisition will take place upon completion of relevant procedures, and the actual acquisition dates for the CB Conversion Transaction and the Share Purchase Transaction may differ from each other.

 

    

The details set forth above may be subject to change depending on the implementation schedule and other factors. The authority to determine the detailed schedule, agreements and other matters relating to these transactions has been delegated to the Company’s representative director.
